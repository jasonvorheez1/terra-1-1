About

        
      

      

        
Terra Ambulate reconstructs any point on Earth from open data
        and lets you walk around it, at 1.4 metres per second, in real time.


        
Where the world comes from

        
    
          
  • Buildings, streets, tunnels, bridges, walls, trees, benches —
              OpenStreetMap,
              queried live through the Overpass API. Heights, storey counts, roof shapes,
              materials and colours are read from the tags where mappers have supplied
              them, and inferred from building type and footprint where they have not.
    • 
          
  • Terrain — the AWS
              Terrain Tiles
              open dataset (Mapzen terrarium encoding, from SRTM and national elevation models).
    • 
          
  • Vegetation — NASA
              GIBS
              MODIS Terra 16-day NDVI. The satellite's vegetation index decides how densely
              the world is planted, how green it reads, and which biome the species palette
              comes from. A parched August park is parched here too.
    • 
          
  • Ground appearance — OSM land cover and NASA biome data select
              generated grass, soil, gravel, sand or snow detail by default. This avoids
              painting overhead cars, shadows and duplicate roads under the 3D world. Esri
              World Imagery remains available as the optional Aerial photograph mode; there it
              is also aligned to flat roofs. Facades stay generated because they are not
              visible from above.
    • 
          
  • Place search — OpenStreetMap Nominatim.
    • 
          
  • The sun and moon — computed, not faked: the NOAA solar position
              algorithm, from the real date, time and coordinates. Midsummer in Svalbard
              genuinely never gets dark.
    • 
        


        
What is invented

        
Interiors are procedurally generated: OSM knows a building's outline and its
        purpose, not its floor plan. Rooms, corridors, stairs and furniture are laid out
        from a seed derived from the building's OSM id, so a given building looks the same
        every time you walk into it.

        
They load as cells, in the manner of Fallout or Morrowind.
        Buildings are solid from the street; walk up to a front door and press
        E to go in. Only one interior exists at a time, and while you are
        inside it the whole outdoor world is unloaded — hidden, and taken out of the
        collision layer the character is tested against. That is a deliberate
        performance decision: a city block holds dozens of enterable buildings, and
        keeping them all resident would cost geometry and collision memory for rooms
        nobody is standing in. Loading one on demand drops the physics from a few
        hundred thousand triangles to a few thousand, which is what lets an interior
        afford seven floors of rooms, stairs and furniture.

        
Facade textures, pitched roof tiles, foliage, street furniture and ambient sound are
        all generated at load time. The game ships no image or audio files.

        
Each building also gets the architectural trim its type calls for: a chimney and a
        porch on a house, a cornice and balconies on a block of flats, a fascia and an awning on
        a shop, a covered entrance on a school, a portico on a civic building, a belfry and spire
        on a church.


        
Please be kind to the servers

        
Overpass, Nominatim and GIBS are free community and public infrastructure.
        Everything downloaded is cached locally, and requests are rate limited. If a
        map server is busy the game fails over to a mirror and keeps walking.
